(clause → customer ask → fallback → vendor position); side panels for "indemnity carve-outs"

## Title
Negotiating the AI deal: asks, fallbacks, and where the risk lands

## Central table — negotiation positions
| Clause | Customer ask | Fallback |
|--------|--------------|----------|
| IP indemnity | Uncapped, duty to defend, training-data + output | Carve out of the general liability cap |
| No-training | All tiers; "Training" defined separately | Contractual opt-out + zero retention |
| Data-rights ladder | Vendor never trains on our data | opt-out → "to provide the service only" → zero retention |
| Model deprecation | 6–12 mo notice, pinning, migration support | 90 days + parallel-run period |
| AUP | Notice-and-cure; material-change notice; no retroactivity | Cure period for non-egregious breaches |
| Warranty | Narrow express warranty + conformity commitment | Documentation-conformance only |

## Side panel — indemnity carve-outs to check
- Claims from your fine-tuning data, prompts, or RAG content — usually excluded
- Patents — often excluded (coverage limited to copyright)
- Conditions that void it: disabling filters, off the AUP, old model version, knowing infringement

## Side panel — the AI insurance gap
- CGL and tech E&O policies increasingly exclude AI-related harms
- So the negotiated liability cap IS the risk allocation — that's why vendors fight for low caps

## Footer — flow-down / embedded model
You can't grant downstream more than your upstream license allows. Watch the indemnity delta: broad promise out, narrow (copyright-only, input-carve-out) protection in.
