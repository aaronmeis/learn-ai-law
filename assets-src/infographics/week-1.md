## Title
Foundations: how to read the law, and the shape of AI law in 2026

## Quadrant 1 — Sources of law, ordered by binding force
- Statute (legislature) → Legislative rule (agency, notice-and-comment) → Executive order (executive branch only, revocable) → Agency guidance (persuasive, withdrawable) → Common law (judge-made)
- Note: *Loper Bright* (2024) ended *Chevron* deference — courts now read statutes independently

## Quadrant 2 — How to read a case / a contract
- Case: facts → issue → holding → reasoning → disposition; note the court and the year; is it binding here?
- Contract: find the stack (MSA / order form / SOW / SLA / DPA / AI addendum); legal terms live in the MSA
- Always separate settled law from open questions

## Quadrant 3 — The four IP regimes (100-level)
- Copyright — human-authored original works; automatic on fixation
- Patent — novel, non-obvious inventions; natural-person inventor; 20 years
- Trade secret — valuable + kept secret; the practical mode for model weights
- Trademark — source identifiers; against consumer confusion

## Quadrant 4 — The US + EU landscape (100-level)
- US: no omnibus AI statute; EO 14179 + America's AI Action Plan (2025) set policy; enforcement via FTC Act §5, ECOA, Title VII
- EU: AI Act (Reg 2024/1689) — risk tiers; GPAI duties live since Aug 2025; high-risk deferred to 2 Dec 2027 (Digital Omnibus)
- States in flux: Colorado AI Act stayed; Texas TRAIGA live; California SB 53 / AB 2013 live
