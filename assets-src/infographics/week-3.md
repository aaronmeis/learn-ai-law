# Week 3 one-pager — Contracts I: anatomy

**Format:** landscape infographic built around a central "contract stack" diagram (stacked
layers), with callouts pointing to each layer, plus a bottom band of "clauses that matter
for AI". Professional palette, dated "snapshot 2026-09-02". Public exemplars only — no real
agreements.

## Title
Anatomy of a commercial AI agreement

## Central diagram — the contract stack (top to bottom)
- **Order form** — services, quantities, fees, dates (the commercials)
- **MSA (Master Services Agreement)** — IP, liability, warranties, indemnity, term/termination (the durable legal terms)
- **SOW** — scope of a specific engagement
- **SLA** — uptime / performance + service-credit remedy
- **DPA** — data-protection terms (GDPR Art. 28)
- **AI addendum** — AI-specific terms bolted on (Common Paper v1.0 / Bonterms v2.0)

## Callout — where to negotiate
Legal terms belong in the MSA. Don't let them drift into documentation the vendor can change unilaterally.

## Bottom band — clauses that matter for AI (with the customer-favorable baseline)
- Training-data use → no training by default; "Training" defined separately from "service improvement"; zero-retention option
- Output IP ownership → assigned to customer as between the parties (not a copyrightability claim)
- IP indemnity / "copyright shield" → two-part (training-data + output), duty to defend, above the liability cap
- Limitation of liability → cap (often 12 mo fees) with carve-outs for IP, confidentiality, data breach
- Warranty disclaimer → must be conspicuous + specific (UCC §2-316)
- Acceptable-use policy → notice before suspension; no unilateral material changes
- Model deprecation → 6–12 month notice + version pinning + migration support
