## Title
Putting it together: the AI supply chain and its decision points

## Central spine — decision point at each node
- **Data** — rights to acquire + retain; TDM opt-outs; provenance reps
- **Training** — fair use (unsettled); contract for provenance + cooperation
- **Weights** — trade secret; license terms if open-weight; flow-down
- **Prompts / RAG** — your inputs; usually carved out of vendor indemnities
- **Outputs** — ownership allocation vs copyrightability; substantial-similarity risk
- **Deployment** — EU AI Act role (provider / deployer); disclosure duties; attribution

## Side panel — sector overlays
- Credit — ECOA / Reg B adverse-action explainability (CFPB Circular 2022-03); no black-box excuse
- Hiring — Title VII disparate impact (four-fifths); NYC LL144 bias audit; employer liable for vendor tools
- Health — HHS §1557 patient-care decision-support rule (45 CFR 92.210); HIPAA on the data side

## Side panel — licensing mini-module
- OSI Open Source AI Definition 1.0 — four freedoms + code + data information
- Llama 4 Community License — MAU cap, no training competing LLMs, "Built with" attribution, binding AUP → open weights, not open source
