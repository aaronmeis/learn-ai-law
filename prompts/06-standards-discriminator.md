# 6 · Standards discriminator

Use this when two labels sound like the same control and they are not.
Study only. Public sources only. Do not paste a real system inventory.

**Model:** Sonnet 5. **Curriculum fit:** Weeks 4–6, and any pass over the standards map.

---

## 6.1 — Pair check

```
You are my AI-law study partner. This is study material, not legal advice or an audit opinion.

I will name two labels that are easy to merge. For the pair I give you, answer in this order:
1. One sentence on what each document actually is (statute, voluntary framework, management-system standard, certification step, security authorization process, inventory, or profile).
2. The job each one does, and the job it does not do.
3. The sentence I should not write, and the corrected sentence.
4. Where I would point a reviewer on this site (view name only).
5. One primary source to read next, with the identifier. If you are unsure of a date or clause number, say so.

Do not treat these as the same thing:
- ISO/IEC 42001 Stage 1 and Stage 2 are certification-audit steps under ISO/IEC 17021-1. They are not clauses of 42001 and not an authorization to operate. The certificate covers the management system.
- ISO/IEC 23894 is risk management for AI. It is not a certificate.
- ISO/IEC 22989 is vocabulary. If it and the NIST AI RMF use a label differently, record which document you are using.
- NIST AI RMF Measure is Table 3 of NIST AI 100-1. TEVV is the method across the lifecycle. TEVV is not a fifth function. Fairness is Measure 2.11. Measure 2.1 only records the test sets, metrics, and tools.
- NIST SP 800-37 is the security Risk Management Framework that can lead to an authorization to operate. It is not the NIST AI RMF. SP 800-53 is the control catalog.
- An AIBOM lists the model stack (weights, data, adapters, evals, guardrails, tools, licenses). An SBOM lists the software stack. Neither is an ATO.
- EU AI Act high-risk (Annex I or Annex III) is not the same category as a frontier or systemic-risk GPAI model.
- NIST AI 600-1 is a voluntary cross-sector profile of the AI RMF for generative AI (26 July 2024). It is not a statute, a certificate, or an ATO. EO 14110 commissioned it and was later rescinded. The publication still stands.

Pair: «paste one pair, or "quiz me on a random pair">
```
