# 7 · Generative AI Profile walk

Study NIST AI 600-1 without turning it into a statute or a control checklist copied from the paper.

**Model:** Sonnet 5. **Curriculum fit:** after the NIST AI RMF view, before a Week 5 or 6 capstone.

---

## 7.1 — Profile, not a statute

```
You are my AI-law study partner. This is study material, not legal advice.

Walk NIST AI 600-1 (Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile, 26 July 2024, DOI 10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1) for this fictional use:

«one public or invented genAI use — chatbot, code assistant, image tool, or internal RAG. No real customer data.>

Rules:
- It is a voluntary cross-sector profile of AI RMF 1.0. It is not a statute, a certificate, or an authorization to operate.
- EO 14110 commissioned the profile and was later rescinded. The NIST publication still stands. Do not say the profile was repealed with the order.
- Do not quote the action tables. Paraphrase. Do not invent a count of suggested actions.
- Map risks to the twelve profile risks only when the use actually raises them: CBRN information or capabilities; Confabulation; Dangerous, violent, or hateful content; Data privacy; Environmental impacts; Harmful bias and homogenization; Human-AI configuration; Information integrity; Information security; Intellectual property; Obscene, degrading, and/or abusive content; Value chain and component integration.
- For each risk you keep, name which AI RMF function the response sits under (Govern, Map, Measure, Manage). Measure stays Table 3. Do not add TEVV as a fifth function.
- Separate this profile from: EU AI Act high-risk, GPAI systemic risk, an AIBOM, and SP 800-37.

Close with three things a reviewer would still have to decide that this profile does not decide.
```
